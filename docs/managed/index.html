Planned commercial offering

From a corpus to a continuously improving system

Managed CoreCortex is intended to operate the expensive, repetitive work around ingestion, evaluation, and storage optimization.

Planned capabilities

  • Maintained source connectors, refresh schedules, and pipeline operations
  • Schema and storage discovery from a representative corpus sample
  • Automatic drafting and review workflows for evaluation examples
  • Continuous experiments across chunking, mappings, indexes, and retrieval plans
  • Promotion gates based on measured quality, latency, cost, and policy
  • Operational monitoring, governance, and enterprise controls

The research loop

Inspired by the experiment discipline popularized by Karpathy's autoresearch project, the planned loop changes one corpus strategy at a time, builds it, runs fixed evaluations, records the result, and keeps only measurable improvements.

The target is not a universally best database. It is a defensible storage and schema strategy for this corpus, workload, and set of constraints.

What remains undecided

Product direction

SaaS is one delivery option

The managed delivery model, packaging, and availability are future decisions. We are currently designing the capability with early partners rather than advertising a generally available service.

Good design-partner fit

  • You operate a consequential corpus whose answers need defensible evidence.
  • Your ingestion sources or access rules change often.
  • You need to compare multiple storage and retrieval strategies.
  • You already have domain experts who can judge useful evidence.
